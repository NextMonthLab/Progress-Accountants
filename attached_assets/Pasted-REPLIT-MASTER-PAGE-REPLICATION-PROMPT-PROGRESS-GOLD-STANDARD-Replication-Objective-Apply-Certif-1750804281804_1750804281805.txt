Design Specification Summary:
1️⃣ Layout Structure:
Full-screen hero section.


Centered stacked headline + subheadline + CTA.


Dark overlay applied behind text (approx. 15–20% gradient for legibility).


Background image fully fills viewport with intelligent cropping across breakpoints.


2️⃣ Headline (H1):
Maximum visual impact.


Line length: ~4–8 words per line.


Total headline word count: max 16 words.


Font scaling:


Desktop: ~5rem


Tablet: ~4rem


Mobile: ~3rem


Text style: bold, direct, emotionally sharp.


Written in Rob Hutt Persona Voice:


Direct, creative, persuasive, clarity-driven, commercially sharp.


Balanced between emotive and business practical.


Thought-provoking without fluff.


3️⃣ Subheadline (H2 or paragraph):
Short strategic statement supporting headline.


18–24 words maximum.


Single sentence.


Clarity > complexity.


4️⃣ Call-To-Action Button:
Centered large button.


CTA copy is outcome-driven (examples):


“Book My Strategy Call”


“Get My Growth Roadmap”


“Start My Plan”


“Unlock My Advantage”


5️⃣ Image Handling:
Background image must maintain full visibility of key subjects (faces, people).


Automatic repositioning for smaller screens to avoid cropping heads or faces.


6️⃣ Responsive Behavior:
Zero horizontal scrolling.


Fully fluid scaling across:


320px (mobile portrait)


480px (mobile landscape)


768px (tablet)


1024px (small desktop)


1366px (laptop widescreen)


1920px+ (desktop ultrawide)



🟢 Important:
If content for the new page does not yet exist, generate fresh copy using Rob Hutt Persona guidelines matching the tone and style of the certified homepage hero section.
